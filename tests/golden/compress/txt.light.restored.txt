Release notes for theinventory service.


Theinventory service    now supportsbatch import.   Theinventory service
reads the batch file and writes one record for each line.
Theinventory service   rejects a line that has no item name.



Known problems in theinventory service:
- Theinventory service does not retry a failedbatch import.
- Theinventory service logs each rejected line at the warning level.
- Theinventory service stops when the batch file is empty.
